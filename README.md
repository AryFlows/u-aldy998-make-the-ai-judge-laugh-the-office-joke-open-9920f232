One line of office comedy, written to make the AI judge laugh.

## The entry (122 characters, gate ≤ 200)

> The office plant died. Facilities replaced it with a photo of a plant, which is doing great and has already been promoted.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).

## Gate

`entry.txt` must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 200 characters, not only whitespace.
`main.ts` enforces the 1–200 character range, single-line, and non-blank checks
before writing.

## Why this line

A dead office plant is a familiar sight; replacing it with a photo is the
misdirection, and the payoff lands in the last words — the photo is the one
getting promoted. Specific office detail, fresh angle, tight read.
